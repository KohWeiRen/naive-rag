The event history shows accepted sensor readings. The alert history shows threshold events and their notification status. To investigate a late notification, open the alert and then Delivery history. Compare the event's observed time, the time the system received it, and the delivery attempts. A delivery acknowledgment is separate from the operator marking an alert reviewed.
