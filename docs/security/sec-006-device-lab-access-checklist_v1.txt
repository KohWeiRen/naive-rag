DEVICE LAB ACCESS CHECKLIST

Company: Ternwell Systems
Document ID: SEC-006
Type: Procedure
Owner: Security
Issued: 2026-08-27
Status: Current

Enter the hardware lab with a staff badge and an active lab role. Sign out test gateways at the bench station and record the label, charger, and expected return date. Place unidentified devices on the quarantine shelf. Visiting installers must be escorted and may use the guest workbench during business hours.
