Intake

The sponsor submits the vendor's business purpose, data classification, hosting region, and estimated spend. Finance owns commercial approval. Security owns review of access, retention, and data handling.

Due diligence

For customer or employee data, request a current security assurance package, a data processing agreement, and a list of relevant subprocessors. Confirm deletion procedures and the vendor's incident contact. Internal-only tools may qualify for a short review.

Decision

Record approved use and restrictions in the vendor register. Reopen the review if the service adds a new data category, changes hosting region, or receives broader permissions.
