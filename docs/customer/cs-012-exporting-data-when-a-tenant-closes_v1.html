The tenant administrator requests a final export before service ends. Confirm the tenant, data category, date range, and regional delivery location. An ordinary final export follows the product retention limits in GOV-002.

Transfer

Provide the export through an approved short-lived link. Confirm the customer's receipt and record the checksum for large deliveries. Support routes deletion requests to Privacy using the case workflow in GOV-004.
