# Customer incident communication templates

Company: Ternwell Systems
Document ID: CS-007
Type: Template
Owner: Customer Enablement
Issued: 2026-09-30
Status: Current

## Initial update

State the affected service and region. Describe what the customer might observe and which facts are confirmed. Provide the time of the next update in UTC. Where unaffected capabilities are relevant, name them plainly.

## Progress update

Explain the current workaround and any change in scope. If the team has mitigated the cause while a queue drains, distinguish those two facts. Avoid an estimated completion time unless the incident commander has validated it.

## Resolution

State when the affected workflow recovered. Mention delayed actions that customers still need to check, such as a webhook receiver that may have timed out. Reference the support case and the follow-up owner.
