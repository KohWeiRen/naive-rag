CHANGING A BILLING CONTACT

Company: Ternwell Systems
Document ID: CS-011
Type: FAQ
Owner: Customer Enablement
Issued: 2026-10-09
Status: Current

A tenant administrator can update the billing contact from Fleet Console settings. Finance verifies the new address before the next invoice run. Open a support case for a change to the contracting entity or tax registration. The billing contact can receive invoices without access to telemetry.
