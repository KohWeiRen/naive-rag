# Regional hosting and data residency

Company: Ternwell Systems
Document ID: GOV-003
Type: Policy
Owner: Privacy and Governance
Issued: 2026-09-05
Status: Current

Wayline provisions tenants in either Singapore or the EU according to the signed contract. Ingest, raw storage, backups, and report exports stay in that contracted region. Support staff may access approved diagnostic views from their work location under SEC-002 when the contract permits it.

## Exceptions

Data migration or restoration outside the contracted region requires a documented exception approved by Privacy, Security, and the account owner. Notify the customer where the agreement requires it. Keep the planned duration and deletion evidence in the request.

## Pilot checks

Confirm region at kickoff and again before enrolling the first gateway. Test the connection endpoint against a synthetic tenant before accepting production sensor data.
