## Company profile

Ternwell Systems is a fictional company that builds Wayline, a platform for monitoring temperatures and humidity across cold-chain facilities and shipments. The Singapore hub covers product, platform engineering, and regional operations. The Rotterdam hub covers EU field operations and customer enablement. Wayline data is hosted in Singapore or the EU according to the tenant contract.

## Core teams

People Operations owns employment workflows, onboarding, and workplace support. Platform Engineering owns the tenant API and webhook dispatcher. Fleet Engineering owns gateway enrollment, firmware, and device diagnostics. Site Reliability runs the duty schedule and restoration exercises. Customer Enablement owns customer onboarding and first-line issue triage.

## Decisions and records

The project owner records decisions with a date and responsible team. Keep customer data in approved systems and use synthetic examples for training. Use SEC-001 for classification, GOV-002 for retention, and OPS-001 for production incident coordination.
