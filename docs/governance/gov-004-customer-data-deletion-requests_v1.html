Verify that the requester is authorized for the tenant. Record the requested scope, region, and date. Distinguish tenant closure, targeted correction, and complete deletion. Ask Legal to check for an applicable hold before sending the work to service owners.

Execution

Confirm the record identifiers and use the approved deletion workflow. Capture counts and verification results. The active-data deadline is defined in GOV-002. If a backup restore is needed before rotation removes the older copy, confirm that the deletion list is replayed.

Closure

Privacy reviews completion evidence and records the customer confirmation date. Record separately when the backup retention window ends.
