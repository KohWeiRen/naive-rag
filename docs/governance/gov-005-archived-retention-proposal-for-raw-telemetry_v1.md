## Status

Superseded draft. The team did not adopt this document. The current schedule is GOV-002, effective 1 October 2026.

## Historic proposal

An early capacity estimate assumed raw telemetry could be retained for 180 days. The team also considered keeping snapshots for 45 days to simplify incident recovery. Those assumptions were used only to compare storage costs during planning.

## Outcome

Privacy selected the shorter retention windows in the approved policy after reviewing contractual requirements and storage usage. The draft is retained so decisions can be traced. Reference GOV-002 for present-day retention and deletion answers.
