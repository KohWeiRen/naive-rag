# Learning and certification allowance

Company: Ternwell Systems
Document ID: PPL-005
Type: Policy
Owner: People Operations
Issued: 2026-09-01
Status: Current

## Allowance

Permanent employees can request up to SGD 1,200 or EUR 800 per calendar year for approved learning. The allowance is a local budget cap. It covers course fees, exam fees, and directly related books. Renewal fees are assessed against the current year. Unused amounts expire in December.

## Approval

Submit the course outline, cost, and expected application to your work before purchasing. The manager approves relevance and coverage for scheduled training days. Finance checks the budget code and reimburses through the usual expense process in FIN-001.

## Completion

After an external course, add one practical takeaway to the team knowledge base. A completed exam result can be entered in the skills register if the employee chooses. People Operations tracks program participation for budget planning.
