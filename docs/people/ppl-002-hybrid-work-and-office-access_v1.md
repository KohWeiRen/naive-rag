# Hybrid work and office access

Company: Ternwell Systems
Document ID: PPL-002
Type: Guide
Owner: People Operations
Issued: 2026-08-03
Status: Current

## Office routine

Product and engineering teams coordinate two collaboration days each week. Site leads set their own anchor days. The Singapore hub reserves Tuesday for cross-team reviews. Rotterdam uses Wednesday for customer operations and hardware handoffs.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely within their approved employment location. Requests to work temporarily from another country go to People Operations at least 20 working days before departure. Approvals depend on employment, tax, insurance, and access restrictions. The IT desk can arrange a loan device for travel.

## Visitors

Register visitors by 16:00 on the previous working day. A host must meet the visitor at reception and escort them in areas used for device testing. Return visitor badges when leaving. Hardware lab access is managed separately under SEC-006.
