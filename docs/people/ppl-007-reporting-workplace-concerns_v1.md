# Reporting workplace concerns

Company: Ternwell Systems
Document ID: PPL-007
Type: Policy
Owner: People Operations
Issued: 2026-08-20
Status: Current

Employees can contact their manager, People Operations, or the confidential reporting channel listed in the People Portal. Reports may concern conduct, safety, discrimination, conflicts of interest, or misuse of company systems. Provide dates, locations, relevant messages, and the outcome you are seeking if known.

## What happens next

People Operations acknowledges receipt within two working days, assigns a case owner, and explains the next update. Access to the record is limited to people needed to assess or address the concern. Any retaliatory behavior should be reported through the same channel.

## Immediate risk

Contact the site safety lead or local emergency services for an immediate threat to physical safety. Report suspected compromise of accounts or data to the Security duty contact under SEC-007.
