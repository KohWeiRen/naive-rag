Coordination

Teams publish a four-hour collaboration window in their shared calendar. The engineering handoff between Singapore and Rotterdam runs at 09:00 Rotterdam local time on weekdays. Each employee records agreed coverage if a customer or on-call role has fixed obligations.

Exceptions

A recurring schedule adjustment needs manager approval and should be reviewed after 60 days. Managers consider customer commitments, reasonable accommodations, and local rules. A temporary one-off schedule change may be agreed directly with the manager.

Time recording

Employees required to report hours use the People Portal. For overnight incident shifts, the duty manager coordinates rest and hands off follow-up work to the next scheduled owner.
