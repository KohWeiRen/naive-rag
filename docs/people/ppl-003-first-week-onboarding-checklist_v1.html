Before the start date

The manager opens the joiner request five business days before the start date and names a buddy. IT assigns a managed laptop and sends a setup invitation through the verified contact channel. People Operations confirms employment records in the portal.

Day one

Pick up the laptop or confirm courier receipt. Enroll a passkey, set up the password manager, and verify access to mail, calendar, and the help desk. The buddy walks through the fictional Wayline product using a demo tenant.

By Friday

Complete security and data handling training. Confirm access to only the repositories and tools your role requires. Practice filing a support request. The manager closes the checklist after reviewing the employee's role and work location.
