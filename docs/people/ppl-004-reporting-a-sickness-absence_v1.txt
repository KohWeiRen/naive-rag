REPORTING A SICKNESS ABSENCE

Company: Ternwell Systems
Document ID: PPL-004
Type: Procedure
Owner: People Operations
Issued: 2026-07-01
Status: Current

Notify your manager through the usual team channel before your expected start time if practical. Record the absence under Sickness in the People Portal when you return or when People Operations requests it. Your calendar can simply say Unavailable. Send medical certificates through the restricted portal upload when required. People Operations manages any longer-term adjustment with the employee and manager privately.
