Objectives

For the core tenant API, the availability objective is 99.9 percent over a rolling 30-day window. For ingest, 95 percent of accepted batches should reach the processing store within two minutes. For webhook delivery, track queue age and external acknowledgment rate by region.

Exclusions

Report customer endpoint failures separately from dispatcher failures. Exclude scheduled maintenance only when the service agreement allows it and the notice requirement was satisfied.

Review

The SRE duty lead reviews error budget burn weekly and after major incidents. Service owners propose remediation when a sustained trend exceeds the team's agreed threshold.
