# Purchasing approval thresholds

Company: Ternwell Systems
Document ID: FIN-002
Type: Policy
Owner: Finance
Issued: 2026-09-01
Status: Current

## Thresholds

A budget owner can approve planned purchases under SGD 2,500 or EUR 1,700. Purchases at or above that threshold require Finance review before an order is placed. Purchases above SGD 15,000 or EUR 10,000 also require approval from the department director.

## Process

Attach a quote and record the budget code, vendor, renewal terms, and expected delivery. Use the vendor intake workflow for a new service. Security review may apply if the vendor will access company data. FIN-005 describes invoice matching once the goods or services are received.
