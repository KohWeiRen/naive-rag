# Invoice matching and payment disputes

Company: Ternwell Systems
Document ID: FIN-005
Type: Procedure
Owner: Finance
Issued: 2026-09-18
Status: Current

Match an invoice to the purchase order and goods receipt. Verify the vendor entity, tax details, currency, unit count, and total. A price or quantity difference requires confirmation from the buyer. Record partial receipts separately.

## Disputes

Mark disputed invoices as on hold in the ledger and tell the buyer which line failed. Ask the vendor for a credit note or corrected invoice. Do not approve a replacement until the original reference is linked so the invoice cannot be paid twice.
